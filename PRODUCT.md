# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, DBAs, analysts, and open-source contributors who work in databases every day. They are technically fluent, often switch between local, staging, and production systems, and want a focused desktop tool that respects their time and data.

## Product Purpose

Cellar is a fast, open-source desktop database client for browsing schemas, running SQL, editing data, and reviewing changes before commit. The website should make the product feel credible, unusually considered, and worth downloading while clearly communicating its local-first, privacy-respecting model.

## Brand Personality

Mechanical, assured, and alive. Cellar should feel like a precision instrument with a pulse: serious enough for production work, distinctive enough to remember, and direct enough for experts.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, soft gradient blobs, floating glass cards, fake dashboards, crypto-neon styling, terminal cosplay, and claims that overstate unfinished features. Do not present Cellar as cloud software, an AI wrapper, or a simplified tool for non-technical users.

## Design Principles

- Show the real product instead of illustrating it abstractly.
- Make trust tangible through specific product behavior and honest status language.
- Use motion to express speed and depth, never to delay access or obscure content.
- Let the interface feel dense and expert without becoming visually noisy.
- Treat open source and local-first operation as product strengths, not footer disclaimers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard access, strong focus states, sufficient contrast, semantic structure, and usable layouts from small phones through wide desktop screens. Respect reduced-motion preferences by removing parallax and nonessential transitions while keeping all content available.
