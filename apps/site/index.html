Privacy by default

Your data stays on your machine.

Cellar is a local-first desktop app. No account, no cloud sync, no phone-home. The only network calls are to your databases and, if you opt in, your own AI provider.

  • BYO key, coming soon. AI provider keys are designed to be stored in the OS keychain and used directly. Cellar must not proxy your prompts through a hosted service.
  • Inspectable context, coming soon. AI context should be visible and controllable before it ever leaves your machine.
  • Telemetry off. Analytics are opt-in and disabled by default. Inspect exactly what's stored locally.
